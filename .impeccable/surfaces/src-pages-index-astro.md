OWN-WORLD: Flat spot inks only: flare orange #F38020 flooding whole regions, ink #141414, label white, aluminium grey. Condensed signage grotesk (Barlow Condensed) in heavy caps for labels, Barlow for body, JetBrains Mono for commands.
